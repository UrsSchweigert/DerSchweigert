This approval is given by the operator and does not constitute certification by independently retained legal or tax counsel. The service remains in pre-launch phase; paid functionality stays disabled until configuration and a separate explicit launch approval occur.

1. Payment Terms

Arithmo Slayer offers an optional subscription to unlock advanced game levels. The subscription costs €3.00 gross (depending on tax status) every 3 calendar months (not per year).

The subscription automatically renews for each new three-month period unless you cancel before your current subscription ends. Gifted subscriptions (e.g., 3 months without a card) do not auto-renew.

Subscribers who registered their accounts before market launch receive free access to all content for as long as the service operates.

1.1 Child Safety and Legal Guardians

Arithmo Slayer can be played by children. If you are under 18 years old, a legal guardian must manage the subscription. The guardian is financially responsible for all payments.

1.2 Cancellation and Refunds

You can cancel your subscription anytime through account settings or this webpage. Cancellation takes effect in your current subscription period; you do not lose access you have already paid for.

Refunds for unused periods are processed after review by our support team and payment provider. Contact us at info@derschweigert.de if you need a refund. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected by this discretionary refund practice.

1.3 Payment Method and Taxes

Payments are processed through Stripe, a secure payment provider. Prices are gross prices and include all taxes applicable to your location.

Stripe collects a billing address during checkout and checks the billing country (Germany). This billing address is not stored as a profile field in your game account. Only verified accounts can purchase paid content.

2. Privacy and Payment Data

2.1 General Data Collection

Arithmo Slayer collects the following information:

  • Email address and password (bcrypt hashed)
  • Game progress and scores (stored server-side in our database)
  • Access to premium content (stored in our database)

2.2 Payment and Subscription Data

When you purchase a subscription, Stripe (our payment processor) directly processes your payment information. Arithmo Slayer stores only the following:

  • Stripe customer ID (unique identifier, no name or card)
  • Subscription and payment status plus renewal/expiration dates
  • Identifiers of completed Stripe events (to avoid duplicate processing)

Stripe collects the billing address during checkout and uses it to check the billing country; it is not stored as a profile field in your game account.

Credit card numbers are NEVER stored in Arithmo Slayer. Stripe manages your payment data according to their privacy policy.

2.3 Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. Required billing records (personal billing data, not anonymized records) may be retained after account deletion for the applicable statutory retention periods; the exact duration and deletion procedure are still subject to review before paid launch.

You can delete your account anytime through account settings. This removes your game progress and other personal account data, except where a statutory retention obligation applies to billing records.

2.4 Event Notifications from Stripe

Arithmo Slayer automatically receives notifications from Stripe about:

  • Payments and invoices (e.g., invoicing, successful payment)
  • Subscription events (e.g., creation, update, cancellation)
  • Refunds
  • Disputes or chargebacks

This data is used to manage your subscription and control access to premium content. It is not shared with third parties.

3. Account Termination and Liability

Arithmo Slayer reserves the right to terminate accounts or services with reasonable notice for legitimate reasons (e.g., abuse, security). You can cancel your account anytime.

The service is provided "as is" without warranty of uninterrupted availability. The service may require updates or maintenance.

4. Applicable Law

These terms are governed by German law. For consumers, statutory consumer protections under the BGB (German Civil Code) apply.

5. Contact and Complaints

If you have questions about your subscription, payments, or privacy, contact us at info@derschweigert.de.

For privacy complaints, you can contact the data protection authority of your state or region.
