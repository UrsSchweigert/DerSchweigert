1. Payment Terms

Arithmo Slayer offers an optional subscription to unlock advanced game levels. The subscription costs €3.00, all applicable taxes included, every 3 calendar months (not per year).

The subscription automatically renews for each new three-month period unless you cancel before your current subscription ends. Gifted access codes (e.g., 3 months without a card) do not themselves create a subscription and do not auto-renew. If you redeem such a code while a paid subscription is already active, the code extends your access and only postpones the date of your subscription's next charge; it does not cancel your existing subscription's automatic renewal, which continues unchanged after the postponed date until you cancel it yourself.

Subscribers whose accounts were registered before 2026-10-15, 14:36:51 UTC (16:36:51 CEST) receive free access to all content for as long as the service operates.

In that case, the guardian, as the account holder, is financially responsible for any subscription on that account.

1.2 Cancellation and Refunds

You can cancel your subscription anytime through account settings or this webpage. Cancellation takes effect in your current subscription period; you do not lose access you have already paid for.

Refunds for unused periods are processed after review by our support team and payment provider. Contact us at info@derschweigert.de if you need a refund. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected by this discretionary refund practice.

1.3 Payment Method and Taxes

Payments are processed through Stripe. Prices are gross prices and include all taxes applicable to your location.

Stripe collects a billing address during checkout and checks the billing country (Germany). This billing address is not stored as a profile field in your game account. Only verified accounts can purchase paid content.

2. Privacy and Payment Data

2.1 General Data Collection

Arithmo Slayer collects the following information:

  • Email address and password (bcrypt hashed)
  • Game progress and scores (stored server-side in our database)
  • Access to premium content (stored in our database)

2.2 Payment and Subscription Data

When you purchase a subscription, Stripe (our payment processor) directly processes your payment information. Arithmo Slayer stores only the following:

  • Stripe customer ID (unique identifier, no name or card)
  • Subscription and payment status plus renewal/expiration dates
  • Identifiers of completed Stripe events (to avoid duplicate processing)

Stripe collects the billing address during checkout and uses it to check the billing country; it is not stored as a profile field in your game account.

Credit card numbers are NEVER stored in Arithmo Slayer. Stripe manages your payment data according to their privacy policy.

2.3 Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. Deleting your account at any time through account settings immediately removes your game progress and other personal account data. Any subscription active at that time is also canceled immediately; you immediately lose access to paid or gifted content (there is no continuation until the end of an already-paid period). Certain billing identifiers and a record that the contract ended are kept as a "tombstone" where statutory retention duties for billing/financial records require it; the exact retention period for these billing/financial records is not yet finally determined.

Successfully used (consumed) verification, password-reset, or email-change codes are deleted immediately. Unconsumed codes — whether expired or rejected by a failed redemption attempt — remain until expiry and are then removed by a daily, automated cleanup routine; this routine's goal is deletion no later than 7 days after expiry.

Records of completed, login-free cancellation requests (see the cancellation form) are scheduled for deletion 3 years after the end of the calendar year in which the case was closed; still-open cases and cases under a legal hold are excluded and retained for as long as necessary.

2.4 Event Notifications from Stripe

Arithmo Slayer automatically receives notifications from Stripe about:

  • Payments and invoices (e.g., invoicing, successful payment)
  • Subscription events (e.g., creation, update, cancellation)
  • Refunds
  • Disputes or chargebacks

This data is used to manage your subscription and control access to premium content. It is not shared for other purposes (e.g., advertising); Stripe itself processes this data as our payment processor (see the Privacy Policy). Records of these events, including billing tombstones and gift-code redemption records, are not currently deleted on an automated schedule; this is deliberately held back pending legal classification (financial/tax records, chargeback evidence) and is not yet finally resolved.
