Overview of Data Processing

When using Arithmo Slayer, the following personal data are processed:

  • Account Data: username, email address, password (hashed with bcrypt)
  • Optional Profile Field: first grade year (for leaderboard categorization, not a birthdate)
  • Usage Data: high scores and gameplay history
  • Security Data: Cloudflare Turnstile anti-abuse token and your IP address (for brute-force rate limiting)
  • Pseudonymous Visit Counters: daily hashed visitor fingerprints for page-load estimation (no raw IP/User-Agent stored, for usage analytics, no cookies)
  • Android Beta Waitlist: the Gmail or Googlemail address you enter for the Play test-track invitation, plus the time of your sign-up
  • Session Cookie: refresh_token (httpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict)

Legal Bases and Purposes

Account and Login Data (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — Contract Performance): Your data are necessary to create a user account, authenticate you, perform email verification, and enable account recovery.

Security and Anti-Abuse Data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — Legitimate Interest): We use your IP address and Turnstile tokens to prevent brute-force attacks, spam, and abuse.

Pseudonymous Visit Counters (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — Legitimate Interest): To estimate daily page visitors, we compute a salted, rotating hash daily from your IP address and User-Agent hash — only this computed hash is stored in our visitor counter, never your actual IP address or User-Agent string. This hash changes daily (different salt each day), making it pseudonymous and preventing tracking across days. This serves to count estimated unique page-loads per day and gather usage statistics about the application, not to identify actual people or measure gameplay. Stored records are automatically deleted approximately 400 days after creation via DynamoDB's asynchronous TTL policy. No cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage are used for this purpose.

Cloudflare Turnstile Script Itself (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — Consent): The Turnstile script is only loaded in your browser after you have agreed to it via our cookie consent banner. You can withdraw this consent at any time, with effect for the future, via "Cookie settings" on the game page (/play) (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Payment/Subscription Data and Cancellation Requests (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — Contract Performance or Pre-Contractual Step): This processing serves to administer your subscription or to handle your cancellation request.

Retention of Billing/Financial Records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — Legal Obligation): Certain billing identifiers and records are retained beyond the end of the contract where statutory retention duties (e.g. commercial and tax law requirements) oblige us to do so.

High Scores and Leaderboard Data (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — Contract Performance): These are processed to deliver the game's ranking and leaderboard features. The leaderboard is public: your username, high score, the first grade year you provided (if any), and the date of your personal best are displayed on the homepage for all visitors to see, even without logging in.

Recipients and Data Processors

Amazon Web Services (AWS): Your user data and scores are stored and processed in the AWS region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt), using:

  • DynamoDB (user data, scores)
  • AWS Lambda (processing and API logic)
  • AWS SES (email delivery for verification and password reset)
  • AWS CloudFront (content delivery)

The Frankfurt region refers to data storage (DynamoDB) and processing by Lambda, as well as email sending via SES. AWS CloudFront is architected differently, by design, as a content delivery network: static content may also be served via CloudFront edge locations outside Frankfurt or outside the EU.

AWS is subject to a standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) under Art. 28 GDPR. Learn more: AWS Data Processing Addendum.

Cloudflare Turnstile: The anti-abuse system is operated via Cloudflare. Your interaction with the widget, IP address, and potentially other data are transmitted to Cloudflare. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) under Art. 28 GDPR is in place for processing by Cloudflare. The exact Cloudflare contracting entity and the specific safeguards that apply for any international data transfers are currently not definitively confirmed and are not asserted here; Cloudflare's anycast network may process data on infrastructure outside the EU. Learn more: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Email Delivery: Transactional emails (verification, password reset, email change) are sent via AWS SES; sending them requires disclosing your email address to our processor AWS (SES), under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) per Art. 28 GDPR (see "Recipients and Data Processors" above). Beyond this necessary delivery via AWS SES, your email address is not shared with further third parties and is not used for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Tracking

Session Cookie (refresh_token): This is an essential cookie for managing your login session. It is:

  • HttpOnly (not accessible from JavaScript)
  • Secure (only transmitted over HTTPS)
  • SameSite=Strict (CSRF protection)
  • Valid for 30 days

This cookie falls under the exemption in § 25(2) TDDDG (strictly necessary cookies) and does not require explicit consent.

Cloudflare Turnstile: The Turnstile script is only loaded after your explicit consent via our cookie consent banner. Without your consent, the script is not loaded. You can revisit and change this decision (accept or decline) anytime via the "Cookie settings" link on the game page (/play); the prior decision is stored only locally in your browser (localStorage), not on our server.

We use no analytics, tracking, advertising, or other non-essential cookies.

In addition to the session cookie, Arithmo Slayer uses the following local browser storage (not on our server):

  • localStorage entry ffm_locale: stores your preferred language (German/English) so the interface loads in that language on your next visit. Persists until you change the language or clear your browser storage.
  • localStorage entry ffm_cookie_consent: stores your cookie decision (see above). Persists until you change your decision or clear your browser storage.
  • sessionStorage entry pending_level_record: temporarily stores the result of a level you just completed until it has been successfully submitted to our server. Deleted after successful submission, and in any case ends no later than when you close the browser tab/session.

Data Retention

Your data are retained as long as your account is active. Deleting your account at any time immediately deletes your username, email, password hash, scores, and profile settings. This does not apply to certain billing identifiers and a record of a closed subscription, which may be retained for the reasons described below (see "Payment and Subscription Data").

Rate-limiting data (IP addresses, request counters) is short-lived: each time window typically ends after a short period, and records are additionally removed automatically via a DynamoDB TTL setting. This TTL deletion runs asynchronously in the background on a best-effort basis, typically within a few days after expiry; it is not instant, and there is no fixed, guaranteed upper bound on how long actual deletion may take.

Verification, Password-Reset, and Email-Change Tokens: A token is deleted immediately once you successfully redeem it. Unconsumed tokens — whether expired or rejected by a failed redemption attempt — remain until expiry and are then removed by a daily, automated cleanup routine; this routine's goal is deletion no later than 7 days after expiry.

Application Logs: Technical application logs from our servers (e.g. error and access logs via AWS CloudWatch) are automatically deleted after 30 days per current configuration. These logs are not intended to contain passwords, tokens, or full email addresses; incidental IP addresses or error messages may nonetheless occur.

Payment and Subscription Data (Optional)

If you purchase an optional subscription, the following data are processed:

  • Stripe Customer ID: A unique identifier that Arithmo Slayer receives from Stripe (not a name or card data)
  • Billing Address: Stripe collects the billing address; the backend checks the billing country for Germany-only subscriptions. The billing address is not stored as a profile field in the game account.
  • Subscription and Payment Status: Renewal/expiration date and payment status
  • Event Identifiers: IDs of already-processed Stripe events (used only to avoid duplicate processing, not a full invoice or payment history)

Important: Card and bank account numbers are not stored by the game. Stripe handles payment details. Billing identifiers linked to your game account, access dates, and processing records are personal data, not anonymous data.

Legal Basis (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR): Processing is necessary to fulfill the subscription contract (invoicing, access control to premium content).

Retention Period: Deleting your account immediately cancels any subscription active at that time, and your access to paid/gifted content ends immediately with no continuation to the end of an already-paid period. Certain billing identifiers and a record that the contract ended are kept as a "tombstone" where statutory retention duties for billing/financial records require it. The exact retention period for these billing/financial records (including logged Stripe events and gift-code redemption records) is not yet finally determined; an automated, schedule-based deletion of these financial/audit records is currently held back for legal reasons (financial/tax records, chargeback evidence).

Third Party (Stripe): A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) under Art. 28 GDPR is in place for payment processing via Stripe. However, Stripe does not process the actual payment data exclusively as our processor: for certain purposes (e.g. fraud prevention, Stripe's own compliance with anti-money-laundering and financial-regulatory law, or card network rules), Stripe may, depending on the applicable role, also act as an independent controller; this is governed by Stripe's own applicable terms and privacy policy. The exact Stripe contracting entity and the specific safeguards for any international data transfers are not currently definitively confirmed and are not asserted here; further detail will be added once established.

Cancellation Requests Without Login

When you use the public cancellation form, we store your contact email, the account details or contract references you supply, your cancellation choice and any requested date, and your optional note. We also store a request identifier, submission timestamp, the manually reviewed account association, processing outcome, and outcome-email sending status. These records are used to review, process, and document your cancellation request.

The request is stored separately from your game account; deleting an account does not automatically delete it. A completed case (status "fulfilled" or "no subscription") is scheduled for deletion 3 years after the end of the calendar year in which it was closed. Still-open requests and cases under a legal hold are not automatically deleted and are retained for as long as necessary.

When you sign up for the Android version on the beta page, we store the email address of the Google account you enter (Gmail or Googlemail), the time of the sign-up, and which page the sign-up came from. We use the address solely to invite you to Google Play's test track. This is not a newsletter subscription: the address is not used for advertising and is not shared with third parties. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time by email to info@derschweigert.de without any formal requirement. After the beta ends, or after you withdraw, we delete the address — at the latest once it is no longer needed for the invitation.
