Ground Alignment Calibrator

Red line = ground (GROUND_Y). The goal: each character's actual feet/claws/hooves should just touch the top of the red line — not float above it, not sink below it. Ignore any drawn soft shadow under a fiend's feet; align the character, not its shadow.
Direction: increasing the value (+ / ↑) pushes the sprite further down (use this when feet float above the line). Decreasing (− / ↓) pulls it up (use this when feet sink below the line). Use the zoomed insets below the main canvas — they make a 1px snap obvious; the full-size canvas above is too small to judge precisely by eye.
Background: pick a level in the “Background level” panel (or press [ / ]) to check the feet against that level's own painted floor. The 60 backgrounds do not share one floor line, so a level that looks grounded here may still sit a few pixels off in another.
Ground line: the red line plus the hero and the fiend move together in the “Ground line (this level)” panel (− / + keys). Every level keeps its own line, and “Generate + copy” emits the "groundY" block for levels/manifest.json — that is what the game reads, so what you line up here is what ships.
Working through the 60 levels: tuning is saved in this browser as you go (✎ = tuned here, ● = already in the manifest), so a reload loses nothing; “Next untouched ▶” (n) jumps to the next level that still needs a look, and “Clear tuning” throws the local edits away without touching the manifest. The calibrator reopens on the level you were last on.
Worth a look first — the survey of all 60 backgrounds flagged these as arguable rather than wrong: L03, L21, L31, L34, L42, L48 and L58–L60.
Keyboard: ↑/↓ = fiend ±0.005 (Shift = ±0.02) · ←/→ = previous/next fiend class · PageUp/PageDown = hero ±1px (Shift = ±5px) · [ / ] = previous/next level · −/+ = ground line ±1px (Shift = ±5px) · n = next untouched level. When everything lines up, click "Generate + copy" and paste the two snippets back into entities.js and difficulty-classes.js.
