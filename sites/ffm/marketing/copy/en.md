### Description (4000 max)

```
Arithmo Slayer is a mental-arithmetic adventure in which every correct answer is a sword strike.

You are a swordsman with a borrowed blade, walking six hand-painted paper stages to wake a rune that has gone dark. Slimes, skeleton warriors and a blue dragon stand in the way — and every one of them carries an arithmetic problem above its head. Answer it and you strike. Answer slowly and you take the hit. Answer wrong and the problem comes back at the end of the level.

SIXTY LEVELS, SIX ACTS
From the first village morning to an empty throne: 60 levels across six acts, each act a parallax diorama built from separate painted paper layers. Nothing here is a template — the stages, the 36 fiends and the dragon were drawn for this game.

FOR KINDERGARTEN, PRIMARY SCHOOL AND ADULTS
• Shapes mode counts shapes first: 25 seconds per question, 9 hearts, no score, no end.
• Easy starts the arithmetic: 15 seconds per question, 7 hearts, the campaign opens act by act.
• Medium is the normal run: 10 seconds, 5 hearts.
• Hard is reflex training: 5 seconds, 3 hearts, combos reward streaks.

SPELLS, COMBOS AND A DRAGON
Three spells are earned along the way — a fireball, a lightning ball as the combo finale, and a heal you hold your finger on. Tough enemies get a health bar; the dragon at the end of the campaign gets its own fight.

BUILT FOR A FAMILY
Separate local profiles on the device, each with its own name, scores and level checkpoints. Switching player takes one tap on the title screen. No sign-up, no account, no chat, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

PAY ONCE
The app is a one-time purchase that unlocks levels 7–60 for every profile on the device. No subscription, no renewal, no virtual currency. Levels 1–6 are free to try. The game is fully playable offline — only the purchase and a restore need a connection.

PRIVACY
No account and no tracking. The privacy policy is bundled into the app and linked below.

LANGUAGES
German and English, switchable at any time.

Arithmo Slayer is made by a one-person studio in Germany. If something feels wrong or missing, write to info@derschweigert.de — the answer comes from the person who built it.
```
