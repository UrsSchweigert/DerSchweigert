No data collection

The app collects, transmits and processes no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking. The app works fully offline and never contacts the provider's servers.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app contains no analytics or advertising services, no tracking technologies and no cookies. Personal data is not transmitted to the provider; the app never contacts the provider's servers.

On-device storage

Progress and scores, the cached purchase state, and the language and sound settings are stored only on the device. This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. It is removed when the app is deleted.

In addition, the app stores local family profiles with the player names you enter, together with their best scores, score history, completed levels and practice badges, on the device. Operating-system backups such as Android Auto Backup, iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup may include this locally stored data and restore it to a device; the provider has no access to those backups.

Purchases through Apple and Google

The one-time purchase that unlocks levels 3–60 is processed solely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment, account or address data. The stores' privacy policies apply: Apple and Google.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, no data from children is processed either. Purchases and links that leave the app are protected by a parental gate.
