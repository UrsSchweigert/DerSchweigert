Player Manual

Arithmo Slayer is a mental-arithmetic adventure. Answer problems to defeat fiends, protect your hearts and clear 60 levels. This manual is bundled with the app and works offline.

Campaign progression

The campaign has 60 levels in four chapters of 15. A level is a sequence of up to 30 encounters; defeat every encounter to clear it.

Levels 1–2 are free. One purchase unlocks levels 3–60 permanently.

Levels 1–10 offer five answer choices; from level 11 there are ten. Level 11 uses the arithmetic of level 10 before the number range grows.

A cleared level is saved automatically for the active player, including level 60. Replaying a cleared level never erases that checkpoint. Play continues at the first unfinished level; when all 60 are cleared, play restarts at level 1.

An attempt you leave before clearing is not banked as a cleared level.

Elite fiends appear with a 10% chance, have three hearts and triple the points of each correct hit.

Difficulty

Choose a difficulty for each level on the Play screen.

  • Easy: 15 seconds per question, 7 hearts.
  • Medium: 10 seconds per question, 5 hearts.
  • Hard: 5 seconds per question, 3 hearts.
  • Shapes: count the shapes, 25 seconds, 9 hearts, no score and no end.

After a defeat, “Try This Level Again” restores that difficulty's hearts and question timer but keeps the score checkpoint from the start of the level.

Controls

Choose an answer by tapping or clicking it. With a keyboard, Enter or Space activates the focused answer.

Tap the playfield outside its left quarter, or the Pause button, to pause and resume.

Hold the left quarter of the playfield to heal (see Healing).

Android Back pauses, backs out of the quit confirmation, and exits from the title.

Switch the language between German and English on the title screen; audio settings are in the pause menu.

Timer

Each question counts down with the time of the chosen difficulty.

If time runs out you lose a heart, the correct equation is shown for two seconds, and the fiend retreats to return later in the level.

The timer stops while the game is paused and only runs during active play. Answering faster earns a larger speed bonus.

An elite fiend that survives a hit keeps the remaining time; the next question restarts the timer.

Abilities and the dragon

Correct answers build a combo. Fireball triggers at combo 6, lightning at combo 11, and the dragon at combo 21 with an 80% chance per hit. Only the highest band reached is rolled once; if the dragon roll fails, no lower spell fires.

Fireball and lightning defeat one fiend immediately.

Dragon in the campaign: a successful breath defeats every remaining encounter in the level once. It pays a fixed bonus for each still-unresolved encounter — the level's flawless baseline score divided by its number of encounters — and then finishes the level through the normal level-clear path. The bonus is never paid twice in one attempt.

In training and Shapes the dragon never finishes the level; it only defeats enemies currently on the field. Spell kills count as defeated encounters, not as correct answers, and give no recovery credit. Combo abilities are not available in training or Shapes.

Healing

In campaign multiple-choice levels, hold the left quarter of the playfield for nine seconds to restore one heart. Keep holding while you answer with another finger.

Getting hurt interrupts healing and resets the progress. At full hearts nothing is granted.

Healing does not consume a question and does not change your score. Recovery training has its own separate rule.

Scores, saving and discarding

A correct hit scores the fiend's base value plus a speed bonus of up to 50 and 10 points per combo step; elite hits triple the result. A requeued answer earns no speed or combo bonus.

Clearing a level saves the reached score to the active player's local progress automatically: a per-level best plus the score history.

Quitting in the pause menu opens “Save your score before leaving?”. Save score banks only the reached score and keeps the level unfinished; progress labels it as an unfinished attempt that did not clear the level, and the level is not marked cleared. Discard returns to the title without recording that attempt.

Retrying after a defeat restores hearts and the timer but keeps the level-start score. Accuracy and streak are compared only with the same player's previous clear of the same level and difficulty; the results are personal goals, not a certification.

Shapes and every training mode never record scores.

Family players

The Players screen on the title manages local family players: add, rename, delete and switch. Each player has separate progress, scores and practice badges.

Switch players only on the title screen; switching is blocked while data is being written or a level is starting. At least one player must remain, and deleting a player removes that player's data.

One purchase unlocks levels 3–60 for every player on the device; scores stay with each player. Everything is stored locally on the device and is included in the operating system's device or iCloud backup. There is no account.

Training modes

Training is separate from the scored campaign. Every mode is unscored, never completes campaign levels, never unlocks content and is never compared with campaign replays. Premium training follows the same unlock boundary as ordinary levels.

  • Targeted practice: revisits up to five distinct recent mistakes from a source level. It is untimed and needs missed questions from that level.
  • Short practice: ends after ten encounters.
  • Assisted practice: ends after ten encounters and is untimed.
  • Elite practice: ends after ten encounters, every enemy has at least three hearts, and the timer restarts for each new question.
  • Recovery practice: ends after ten encounters and grants one heart after five distinct correct replies; a corrected retry counts once, at most two hearts are granted per attempt, credit at full health is discarded, and spell kills never count.
  • Shape counting: resolves ten questions and shows the result only after the final correction; Endless Shapes remains available.

Worked examples introduce subtraction, multiplication and division at levels 5, 25 and 34, with Continue or Skip and no score writes.

Practice badges describe activity, not mastery.

Payment and restore

Levels 1–2 are free. The one-time, non-consumable purchase unlocks levels 3–60; there is no subscription and no recurring charge. The store shows the local price and bills Apple or Google. There is no backend and no third-party purchase service.

Purchase and restore first ask an adult to solve a written multiplication (parental gate).

The store reports success, cancellation, pending or Ask-to-Buy, already owned, or an error. A pending purchase unlocks the levels once the store confirms it.

Restore purchase recovers the unlock on a fresh install or a new device with the same store account. The entitlement is cached for offline play, refreshed from the store on launch and resume, and revoked on refund. The unlock applies to the whole device.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent to the provider; only the store's purchase and restore flow uses the network.

Player names, scores, progress and practice badges stay on the device and are included in operating-system backups.
