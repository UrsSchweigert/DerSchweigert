The story

A man with no past, a world of water

He wakes at the bottom of a golden kelp forest, breathing water, with the feeling that he has done something terrible. A mariner girl named Sefa finds him and calls him Wrack, the name her people give to whatever the tide brings in.

Ovara is a vast ocean world. The mariners live in ancient cities under the sea, and those cities are failing. The agravars live on the islands above, ruled by corporations that pour their waste into the water. Into this world comes a stranger who must learn who he was before the sea decides who he will be.

The game follows the novel The Man the Tide Brought In and uses the author’s own words.

New to visual novels?

What is a visual novel?

A visual novel is a story you read on screen, told through illustrated scenes instead of long blocks of text. Characters appear as portraits in front of painted backgrounds, and the words come a few lines at a time, like a book staged for the eye. You set the pace by tapping to go on. There are no puzzles and no reflexes to test. It sits between a novel and a graphic story, and it suits a quiet evening, a commute, or a long read in one sitting.

Support

Help and contact

Something not working, or a question about the game? Write to info@derschweigert.de. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you use, and what happened.

How do I save my place?

To save, open the menu in the game and choose Save. To continue later, choose Load on the main menu.

How do I jump to a chapter?

Choose Chapters on the main menu.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The story and art are in the app.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your saves stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.

I deleted the app and lost my saves.

Saves live on your phone, so deleting the app removes them. They are not backed up by us.
